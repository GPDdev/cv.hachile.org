National Undergraduate Innovation and Entrepreneurship Training Program · Team Member

AegisDrive: World-Model-Assisted Minimum-Risk Decision System for Autonomous-Driving Failures

Uses a dual-track architecture combining in-cabin monitoring and external perception. Eye and mouth geometry rules determine driver state, while a lightweight YOLOv8n model performs vehicle detection and monocular ranging. A world-model-based pipeline processes two video streams concurrently with Python multiprocessing and issues real-time warnings. I developed the in-cabin facial-recognition component and contributed to project documentation. The project won second prize in the freshman division of the 25th Tongluren Transportation Science and Technology Competition, was upgraded to a national-level undergraduate innovation project, and received a silver award in the Tongji University round of the 9th China International College Students' Innovation Competition.

GitHub: AegisDrive → GitHub: IDMS →

SITP Team Member · Embodied Intelligence

QDepth-VLA: Lightweight Vision Models for Orchard Harvesting

Generates pseudo-depth with a monocular depth model, discretizes it into depth tokens through a VQ-VAE, and injects geometric information into the VLA backbone through a Depth Expert and Hybrid Attention. Using only a standard RGB camera, the project validates a closed-loop recognition–localization–harvesting prototype in simulation and on a desktop robotic arm. I handled early literature research, faculty and laboratory coordination, harvesting-method analysis, and robotic-arm development. The work combines two SITP undergraduate innovation projects and is competing in the 15th Challenge Cup Chinese College Students' Entrepreneurship Competition.

First Author · Manuscript Under Review

AI-Based Analysis and Prediction of Lithium-Dendrite Growth in Lithium-Metal Batteries

Constructed a multimodal dataset containing scanning-electron-microscope images, electrochemical time series, and electrolyte and interface descriptors. Used CNNs for dendrite morphology recognition and grading, convolutional and recurrent neural networks to predict polarization voltage and overpotential fluctuations, and regression models to analyze relationships among electrolyte composition, transport properties, and dendrite growth, providing an interpretable screening approach for safer lithium-metal battery design.
